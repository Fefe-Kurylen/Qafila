Qafila (قافلة) means "caravan" in Arabic.

Qafila is a fast, private and fully customizable browser for iPhone. Clean, elegant, with no ads and no tracking.

PUT EVERY BUTTON WHERE YOU WANT IT
• Choose the buttons in the toolbar and in the menu, their order and size
• Toolbar gallery with ready-made layouts: Complete, Navigation, Tools, Reader, Minimal, Zen
• Toolbar at the bottom or top, Classic, Glass or Floating style
• 6 address bar styles, thin, regular or bold icons

BEAUTIFUL, TIDY TABS
• Tabs grouped by site, as a list or a grid of thumbnails
• Sort by date, reading time or title
• Private tabs, never saved in history
• Forgotten tabs close automatically

BROWSE WITHOUT BEING TRACKED
• Built-in blocker for ads, trackers and cookie banners
• JavaScript on or off, per site
• Automatic HTTPS upgrade
• No data collection, no account, no third-party service

READ COMFORTABLY
• Light, dark or automatic mode, and dark mode for every website
• Reader view that extracts the article, text size from 80 to 150%
• Estimated reading time in the address bar and the tab list
• Desktop site, translation, find in page

MAKE IT YOURS
• Toolbar colors for light and dark mode, 8 accent colors
• 5 pyramid app icons
• Search engine of your choice: DuckDuckGo, Qwant, Kagi, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia, SearXNG, Google, Bing or your own
• Available in English, French, Arabic, Spanish and German

Qafila uses WebKit, the engine behind Safari: websites render exactly as you expect.
Every feature is free. An optional "Support Qafila" subscription helps the project and unlocks the exclusive "Gold" icon.
