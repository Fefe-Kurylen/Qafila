Qafila (قافلة) means “caravan” in Arabic.

Qafila is a fast, private and fully customizable web browser.

PUT EVERY BUTTON WHERE IT SUITS YOU
• Choose the toolbar and menu buttons, their order and their size
• Add separators to organize the menu into sections
• Gallery of ready-made bars: Complete, Navigation, Tools, Reader, Minimal, Zen
• Bar at the bottom or the top, Classic, Glass or Floating style

BEAUTIFUL, TIDY TABS
• Tabs grouped by site, as a list or a grid of thumbnails
• Sort by date, reading time or title
• Private tabs, never saved in your history

BROWSE WITHOUT BEING TRACKED
• Built-in ad and tracker blocker
• Cookie banners hidden
• JavaScript on or off, site by site
• Automatic upgrade to HTTPS
• No data collection, no account, no third-party service

READ IN COMFORT
• Dark mode for every site, or site by site
• Reader view that extracts the article
• Estimated reading time
• Bars that hide as you scroll for full-screen reading
• Desktop site, translation, find in page

MAKE IT YOURS
• Interface color that matches the site you visit, 8 accent colors
• Your choice of search engine: DuckDuckGo, Qwant, Kagi, Brave, SearXNG, Google, Bing or your own Qafila instance
• Works as Android's default browser
• Available in English, French, Arabic, Spanish and German

All features are free, with no ads. An optional subscription supports Qafila and unlocks the exclusive Gold icon.
