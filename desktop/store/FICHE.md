UNE BARRE QUI VOUS RESSEMBLE

• Favoris en dossiers, glisser-déposer, barre de favoris discrète

NAVIGUEZ SANS ÊTRE SUIVI
• Bloqueur de publicités, de traqueurs et de bandeaux de cookies intégré

## 🇬🇧 English

**Short description**: A fast, private browser: floating glass toolbar, tidy tabs, built-in blocker, zero data collection.

**Description**

Qafila is a fast, private and fully customizable browser for Windows. Clean, elegant, with no ads and no tracking.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No data collection, no account, no ads, no telemetry
• History, bookmarks and passwords stay on your computer, never sent to Qafila
• Passwords encrypted (AES-256) in a vault only you can open
• Private tabs leave no trace once closed

A TOOLBAR THAT FITS YOU
• Floating glass toolbar, in one or two parts, adjustable transparency
• Ready-made toolbar gallery plus an editor for buttons and menu
• Adjustable sizes: interface, address bar, bookmarks
• Light, dark or automatic theme, toolbar and accent colours

TIDY TABS AND BOOKMARKS
• Tabs grouped by site, as a list or a thumbnail grid
• Bookmark folders, drag and drop, discreet bookmarks bar
• Direct import from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox (bookmarks and passwords)

BROWSE WITHOUT BEING TRACKED
• Built-in blocker for ads, trackers and cookie banners
• Automatic HTTPS, per-site JavaScript setting
• Extensions from the Chrome Web Store

READ COMFORTABLY
• Reader that extracts the article, dark mode for every site
• Estimated reading time, find in page, translation

Available in English, French, Spanish, German and Arabic. Made by an independent developer.

**Features**
- No data collection, no account, no ads
- Encrypted passwords, kept only on your computer
- Fully customizable floating glass toolbar
- Bookmark folders and import from Chrome, Edge and Firefox
- Blocker for ads, trackers and cookie banners
- Private tabs that leave no trace
- Reader, dark mode and Chrome extensions

**Keywords**: browser, private, ad blocker, tabs, bookmarks, passwords, privacy

**What's new**: First release of Qafila on the Microsoft Store.
